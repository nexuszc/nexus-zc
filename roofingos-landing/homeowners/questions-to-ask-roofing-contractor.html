10 Essential Questions to Ask Your Roofing Contractor

Hiring a roofing contractor is one of the biggest home improvement decisions you'll make, and it's natural to feel uncertain about what to ask. The right questions upfront can save you money, prevent delays, and ensure your roof gets the quality work it deserves.

Questions About Experience and Credentials

Before you hire anyone to work on your roof, you need confidence in their qualifications. Ask how long they've been in business—contractors with 5+ years of experience have usually weathered market changes and learned from mistakes. Request proof of licensing and insurance, including general liability and workers' compensation. Ask for references from recent projects, and actually call them. Don't skip this step.

  • How many years have you been in the roofing business?
  • Are you fully licensed and insured in this state?
  • Can you provide at least three references from similar projects?
  • Do you have certifications from shingle or material manufacturers?

Questions About Materials and Warranty

Your roofing materials directly impact your roof's lifespan and your home's protection. Ask which materials they recommend for your climate and why. Different regions benefit from different shingles, membranes, or metals. Understanding warranty terms is equally important—find out whether the warranty covers materials only or labor too, and how long it lasts. Some warranties are transferable if you sell; others aren't.

  • What roofing materials do you recommend, and why?
  • What's covered under warranty, and for how long?
  • Is the warranty transferable to future homeowners?
  • What happens if there's a defect after installation?

Questions About Cost and Payment Terms

Price matters, but the cheapest bid often signals poor quality. Request detailed written estimates from multiple contractors that break down labor, materials, and disposal costs. Ask about financing options if you need them. Never pay the full amount upfront—a typical payment schedule is 25% deposit, 50% midway through, and 25% upon completion. If a contractor demands payment in full before work begins, that's a red flag.

  • Can you provide a detailed written estimate?
  • What's included in your price estimate?
  • Do you offer payment plans or financing?
  • What's your typical payment schedule?
  • Will you handle my insurance claim paperwork?

Questions About Timeline and Project Management

A roof replacement typically takes 1–3 days, depending on size and complexity. Ask for a realistic timeline and what factors could cause delays. Understand how the contractor manages their schedule—do they juggle multiple projects simultaneously, or focus on one at a time? Ask how they'll keep you informed during the work and who your point of contact is if issues arise. Clear communication prevents frustration and surprises.

  • When can you start, and how long will the project take?
  • What happens if weather delays the work?
  • How many crews do you manage at once?
  • Who will I contact with questions during the project?
  • Will you provide daily progress updates?

Questions About Cleanup and Inspections

A professional contractor should leave your property clean. Ask whether they'll remove old roofing materials and haul away debris, or if that costs extra. Clarify what cleanup looks like—will they sweep your roof, gutters, and surrounding areas? Finally, ask if they'll do a final inspection with you and provide documentation of the completed work. This ensures you know exactly what was done and creates a record for future reference.

  • What's included in cleanup after the job's done?
  • Do you remove and dispose of old roofing materials?
  • Will you do a final walkthrough inspection with me?
  • Will you provide photos or documentation of the completed work?

Asking these questions shows you're a thoughtful homeowner, and reputable contractors appreciate working with people who care about quality. Take your time, do your research, and don't hesitate to ask anything else that comes to mind.
